## 1. What this covers

This Cookie Policy explains how LumiDesk (provided by **DeepO3**) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and web app. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](./privacy_policy.md).

## 2. The short version

LumiDesk currently uses **only strictly-necessary cookies** — the small files required to sign you in and keep the app secure. Under the EU ePrivacy rules (and GDPR), strictly-necessary cookies **do not require consent**, so we do not show a cookie banner today. We do **not** use advertising cookies, and we do **not** sell your data.

## 3. Cookies we use

| Cookie | Purpose | Type | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ld_token` | Holds your signed-in session (access token) so you stay logged in | Strictly necessary | Session / until expiry |
| `ld_refresh` | Securely renews your session so it doesn't drop mid-use | Strictly necessary | Up to [30] days |
| `ld_org` / `ld_org_name` | Remembers which business account you're acting in | Strictly necessary | Session |

All of the above are **first-party** cookies set by LumiDesk, marked `HttpOnly` and `Secure` where applicable, and used only to operate the service. [Confirm this list matches the cookies actually set before publishing.]

## 4. If we add analytics or marketing cookies later

We don't use analytics or marketing/tracking cookies today. **Before** we introduce any non-essential cookies (for example product analytics), we will:

1. show a **cookie consent banner** that lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies before they are set, and
2. update this policy to list them, their purpose, provider, and retention.

Until then, no consent banner is needed because only strictly-necessary cookies are in use.

## 5. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but blocking the strictly-necessary cookies above will stop you from signing in and using the app.
