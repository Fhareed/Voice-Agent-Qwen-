The goal isn't to sell in the email. It's to get them to watch a 90-second video and reply "yeah, set it up." Short, plain, human. No corporate voice.

## The 3 emails

Send these in order to the same person. Stop the moment they reply.

### Email 1 — first touch (Day 0)

**Subject:** missed calls at {Business}?

> Hi {Name},
>

## Subject lines to rotate (test which gets opens)

- missed calls at {Business}?
- quick question about your phones
- {Business} — the calls you're missing
- who answers when you can't?
- 90 seconds, then I'll leave you alone

Keep them lowercase-ish and plain. Anything that smells like marketing gets deleted.

## How to find leads (free, ~15 min for 20)

1. Pick ONE metro from the "Target metros" tab. Work it fully before moving on.
2. Google Maps → search **"plumber"** or **"HVAC"** in that city.
3. Skip the big franchises (Benjamin Franklin, Parker & Sons, anything with 50 reviews-per-week). You want **small owner-run shops, 1–5 trucks** — the owner reads their own email.
4. Click each shop → open their website → **Contact / About page** → grab the email and the owner's name. Facebook business pages often have it too.
5. Drop it in the Leads tab: Business, Type, City, Owner, Email, Website. Status stays "New".

Aim for 20 a day. That's it.

## Daily routine (~30 min)

- **Source** 20 new shops into the tracker (15 min).
- **Send Email 1** to the 20 you sourced yesterday. Set Status = Emailed and today's date in "Date emailed" (10 min).
- **Send follow-ups** that are due today — sort the Leads tab by the "Follow-up 1 due" / "Follow-up 2 due" columns (5 min).
- Log any replies (Status → Replied / Demo booked).

Do it 5 days a week and you'll have ~100 shops in the pipeline in a week.

## Don't land in spam (important)

- **Keep volume low from a Gmail:** 20–30 a day, max. Blasting 100 from a fresh account gets you flagged fast.
- **Personalize the first line** — at minimum the business name. Never send something that looks mass-merged.
- **Send Tuesday–Thursday, US morning.** You're in Finland (UTC+2/3), so that's your afternoon/evening.
- **Space them out** — don't fire 20 in one minute.
- **Always keep the "reply stop" line.** It's basic US anti-spam compliance (CAN-SPAM) and it's the honest thing to do.
- When you can afford it, move off Gmail to a real domain (e.g. hello@yourbrand.com) — better delivery and looks legit. Not required to start.

## What to expect (so you don't get discouraged)

Cold email replies run **1–5%**. So ~200 emails → a handful of real conversations → maybe 1–3 who'll try it. That's normal and good. The first "yes, set it up" is the whole game — everything gets easier after one shop is using it and catching real calls.

One quiet reply from one busy guy (hi, Scoopy) tells you nothing. Two hundred emails tells you what's actually working. Volume first, then optimize.
